Temperature Guide

A quick introduction to the three scales you'll encounter.

°C

Celsius

Used in most countries for everyday weather and cooking. Water freezes at 0°C and boils at 100°C.

°F

Fahrenheit

Commonly used in the United States. Water freezes at 32°F and boils at 212°F on this scale.

K

Kelvin

The scientific standard. It starts at absolute zero (0 K), the point where molecular motion stops.
